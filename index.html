Clinical Self-Assessment Screening

PCOS Likelihood Calculator

PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) is one of the most common causes of irregular periods and infertility in women. Answer the questions below honestly to get an instant, private indication of your PCOS likelihood based on well-established symptom patterns.

Are your periods irregular — cycles longer than 35 days, fewer than 8 periods a year, or unpredictable in timing?

Do you have excess hair growth on your face, chest, or back?

Do you have persistent acne or oily skin, especially along the jawline?

Have you noticed unexplained weight gain or difficulty losing weight?

Do you have thinning hair or hair loss on your scalp?

Do you have dark, thickened patches of skin on your neck, armpits, or groin?

Does your mother or sister have PCOS, type 2 diabetes, or a history of irregular periods?

Have you been trying to conceive for more than 6–12 months without success?

Disclaimer

This PCOS Calculator is a self-assessment screening tool based on commonly reported PCOS symptoms such as irregular periods, hirsutism, acne, weight gain, and family history. It is meant only to help you recognise possible warning signs and is not a substitute for a clinical diagnosis. PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) can only be confirmed through a proper medical evaluation, including a hormone panel and pelvic ultrasound, as per standard diagnostic criteria. If your result suggests a moderate or high likelihood of PCOS, or if you are experiencing irregular cycles or difficulty conceiving, we recommend booking a consultation with our fertility & PCOS specialists for accurate diagnosis and a personalised treatment plan.

Clinical Red Flag & Emergency Triage

While PCOD/PCOS is an outpatient endocrine condition, acute complications require urgent evaluation. Use these clinically validated triage screens if you experience severe symptoms.

🚨 Immediate ER

Acute Pelvic Pain & Nausea

Sudden, agonizing, one-sided lower pelvic pain accompanied by nausea or vomiting.

Emergency: Suspected Ovarian Torsion (twisted ovary stalk cutting off blood supply) or Ruptured Cyst with internal bleed.
🚨 Immediate ER

Severe Menstrual Flooding

Soaking through 1+ large pads or tampons every hour for 2+ consecutive hours, golf-ball sized clots, dizziness, or feeling faint.

Emergency: Acute uterine hemorrhage risking hypovolemic shock and severe anemia.
⚠️ Urgent Consult

90+ Days Without a Period

No menstrual period for over 3 months with negative pregnancy tests. Continuous estrogen without progesterone ("unopposed estrogen").

Risk: Endometrial Hyperplasia (abnormal thickening of the uterine lining) and malignant cellular mutation risk.
